### **DIGITAL TWIN**

Let's talk about the thing that 002 left hanging.

At the end of the Zero Trust text, we dropped a line: *"They are not just watching… they are cloning."* We didn't explain it. We're explaining it now.

But first, a detour through the space age. Bear with us.

The concept is older than the name. In the **1960s**, NASA was already building real-time computational models of spacecraft — virtual replicas that engineers on the ground could interact with while the physical object was in flight [49]. They didn't call it anything in particular. It was just how you kept an astronaut alive when the hardware was 400,000 kilometers away and you couldn't send a technician. The most famous case is **Apollo 13**: when the oxygen tank ruptured in 1970, engineers on the ground ran simulations on their physical replica of the spacecraft to find a solution before the crew ran out of time. The twin saved the mission. Nobody used that word yet.

In **2002**, a professor named **Michael Grieves** put a label on it [50]. He called it the **"Digital Twin"**: a virtual replica of a physical object, continuously updated by sensor data, used to simulate, predict, and optimize the behavior of its physical counterpart. General Electric ran with it to monitor jet engines. For twenty years, Digital Twins lived in factories. They modeled turbines. They modeled bridges. They modeled urban drainage systems.

The concept spent sixty years being developed in aerospace and industry before anyone thought to apply it to people. That gap is closing now.

Nobody modeled people.

Until 6G made it cheap, fast, and — for the first time — physically possible at scale.

---

The EU's **Hexa-X** project, the academic flagship designing 6G, lists "Digital Twins of the Physical World" as one of the six foundational use cases of the new network [51]. Fine. Sounds industrial. But read their sub-documents — specifically **Hexa-X-II Deliverable D2.1** — and a different category appears: **"Human Digital Twins"** [52].

Not twins of infrastructure. Twins of individuals.

The pipeline is already described in this series. The **ISAC layer** [001] senses your body through radio waves — your gait, your heartbeat, your location, your breathing. The **Zero Trust layer** [002] scores your behavior continuously — your movement patterns, your typing rhythm, your physical reactions to your environment. In the Zero Trust framing, this data was described as authentication input. Verified, used, discarded.

It is not discarded.

It accumulates. It is fed into a model. The model is updated. The model becomes more accurate. The model starts making predictions. At some point — and the 3GPP specification **TR 22.874** describes this moment without naming it — the network stops reacting to you and starts anticipating you [53]. It pre-configures resources before you request them. It reroutes traffic before you move. It flags anomalies before you act.

This is the **Digital Twin** in operation. Not a record of your past. A simulation of your future.

---

Here is the thing that makes this structurally different from what surveillance capitalism already does.

Google has data about you. A lot of it. But Google's data updates when you *do something* — you search, you click, you watch. There are gaps. You sleep. You walk outside. You have conversations that no microphone captures. These gaps matter. They are the last remnant of a private interior life.

The ISAC sensing layer has no gaps. It operates whether you are active or passive, connected or disconnected, awake or asleep. The **Nokia Bell Labs 6G Vision paper** describes this as "always-on ambient intelligence" [54]. The network does not wait for your input. It observes your existence.

A Google profile is a diary someone else wrote about you.

A Human Digital Twin is a body someone else runs simulations on.

And the data type is different in a way that matters legally and permanently. Google knows what you searched. The twin knows your **cardiac rhythm under stress**. Your **gait signature** — which is as unique as a fingerprint and cannot be changed [55]. Your **micro-behavioral patterns** that you are not consciously aware of yourself. This is not behavioral data. It is biological data. You cannot rotate it like a password. You cannot generate a new one.

---

Now. The GDPR question. Because someone will ask it.

**Article 17** — the Right to Erasure — says you can demand that a company delete your personal data [56]. And let's say they comply. Let's say the twin is deleted.

The ISAC network is still running. The Zero Trust engine is still scoring. The sensing infrastructure does not pause because a deletion order was executed. By the time the legal process completes, the reconstruction is already underway. The deletion and the rebuilding happen in parallel, and one of them is faster.

There is a further problem that legal frameworks have not caught up with: **the model is not the data**. A machine learning system trained on years of your biological signals develops internal weights, parameters, representations — none of which are technically "your data" under current definitions [57]. The law says delete the inputs. It says nothing about the knowledge the system derived from those inputs. The twin can be rebuilt from the model even if the raw data is gone.

You cannot delete yourself from a system that has already learned you.

---

The **ITU-T** formalized all of this quietly in **Recommendation Y.3090** (2022): the international standard for Digital Twin Network architecture [58]. The **ETSI** published **GR PDL 007** describing closed-loop automation systems where the twin predicts and pre-empts user behavior [59]. The **Twin-6G project** — funded by Horizon Europe — is building exactly this, right now, with your tax money [60].

We want to say something about what "pre-empting behavior" means when it leaves the network optimization context and enters a governance context. When an insurance company prices your policy based on your twin's predicted health trajectory, it is not insuring you — it is insuring a simulation of you, a simulation you have never seen and cannot contest. When a hiring system screens your twin, you are not being evaluated — a model of you that you cannot correct is being evaluated in your place. When a court admits behavioral forecasts derived from your twin as evidence of intent [61]...

We will let you finish that sentence.

---

001 established that opt-out is physically impossible.
002 established that anonymity is architecturally impossible.
The Digital Twin adds a third layer: **even erasure is technically meaningless**.

Together, these three properties describe a system with no exit. Not a conspiracy. Not a hypothesis. A funded, standardized, actively deployed infrastructure whose logical conclusion is a world in which every human being has a shadow — a computational shadow, owned by others, that predicts them, represents them, and outlasts them.

**Your twin is potentially immortal. You are not.**

That asymmetry is the one worth fighting.

The battle started today. Enjoy the fight.

Sincerely yours,

**stop6g.eu**
